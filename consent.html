Send your feedback?

LetterBloxd will send this to the developer:

  • the message you typed
  • the extension version, your browser and operating system
  • the kind of page you were on (e.g. “film page”, never its address)
  • your LetterBloxd display settings
Nothing else: no username, no viewing history. Firefox remembers your answer; change it any time in Add-ons.
